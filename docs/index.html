driftwatch

An AI news-trading experiment that keeps its own receipts. Every prediction is written to a hash-chained ledger and anchored in git before the outcome is known, then graded against simply buying the S&P 500.

The tournament

Three paper-money accounts with the same rules and risk limits, each started at $100k, against buy-and-hold SPY. The baseline uses no AI at all: if the AI can't beat a one-line rule, it doesn't deserve real money.

"P(beats luck)" is the Deflated Sharpe Ratio: the chance the Sharpe ratio is real after accounting for 3 strategies being tried at once. The bar for real money is high confidence after roughly 60+ trading days.

Does the AI actually know anything?

Every call the analyst panel makes is graded as the stock's return minus SPY's over the same window, entering at the first price we could really have traded at. A "hit" is a call that leaned the right way.

Recent calls

P(up) is the panel's probability the stock beats SPY over the next 5 trading days. AGREE means all three analyst personas leaned the same way. "Moved" is how far the stock had already moved when the call was made.
